# Procurement, Privacy & Safeguarding Checklist

Before production deployment, document and approve:

- Purpose and lawful/authorized educational use for each data field.
- Data minimization: collect only what the feature needs.
- Student/guardian notice and consent where required.
- Role-based access; least privilege; staff offboarding.
- Encryption in transit and at rest where applicable.
- Retention and deletion schedule, including backups.
- Export/portability and correction/appeal workflow.
- Vendor/subprocessor list and data locations.
- Incident response and breach notification process.
- No sale/advertising profiling using student data.
- No hidden telemetry in the student experience.
- Human review for consequential AI outputs.
- Accessibility conformance and accommodation testing.
- Moderation, safeguarding escalation, and emergency procedures.
- Audit history for high-impact rule/configuration changes.
- Contract exit plan and readable data export.
- Security review of multiplayer signaling/relay services before enabling them for students.

This checklist is implementation guidance, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Schools should map it to their applicable education, privacy, child-safeguarding, records, accessibility, and procurement requirements.
